This policy covers returns of purchased items and the refunds paid for them.

## Returning an item

A customer can return an item within 30 days, with a receipt.[^owner-statement-2026-09-13]

Items must be unused.[^owner-statement-2026-09-13]

## When the refund is paid

Refunds land within 5 working days.[^owner-statement-2026-09-13]

## Faulty goods

Faulty goods are exempt from the 30-day window.[^owner-statement-2026-09-13]
